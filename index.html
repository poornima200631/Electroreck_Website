Who We Are

BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

Electroreck Society is the premier technical and cultural society of the Electrical Engineering Department at NIT Kurukshetra. Founded to bridge the gap between academia and industry, we cultivate a community of engineers who think boldly and build fearlessly.

From organizing flagship technical events to conducting workshops, guest lectures, and industrial visits — we empower students to grow beyond the curriculum and prepare for the challenges of tomorrow.
